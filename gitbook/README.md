# Goals

The Open Trust Registry project aims to provide several key things:

* A reference implementation of policy and technology.
* A set of guidance for two very different types of implementors:
  * Consumers - the Relying Parties that are consuming information from a Trust Registry. They are the folks making trust decisions based on information received (but not exclusively) from a trust registry.&#x20;
  * Providers - the people and organizations that are serving up information backed by their systems of record. They are the authoritative sources of information.
